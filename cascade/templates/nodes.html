How routing works

/s/ stream

Cascade pulls bytes from the node and writes them to the visitor, preserving Range requests so video seeks work. Nothing about the source is exposed.

/f/ forward

A 302 to the resolved direct URL. Best for Dropbox, Drive and TeraBox where the CDN should carry the transfer. Local links fall back to streaming.

Replication

Copies stream node-to-node in the background. Targets must be writable nodes: local disk or FileBrowser.
